## English

**App name:** Hidden Cost

**Short description:** See the real total cost before you buy.

**Full description:**

Hidden Cost helps you understand more than the price tag by estimating the total cost of owning a
product or service over time.

Start with templates for motorcycles, cars, boats, electronics, and subscriptions, or create a
custom calculation. Bring purchase price, financing, fuel or energy, maintenance, recurring costs,
and expected resale value into one explainable result.

Key features:

- Offline calculations and local saving
- Low, base, and high scenarios
- Monthly, per-use, and itemized cost views
- Top cost drivers and a completeness indicator
- Normalized comparison for up to three projects
- PDF reports and shareable result cards
- Turkish/English, light/dark themes, and accessible controls
- Optional cloud backup and anonymous usage analytics

Results depend on the assumptions you enter. Hidden Cost does not provide financial, legal, tax, or
investment advice and does not automatically convert currencies.

**Feature bullets:** Calculate offline · Compare scenarios · Reveal cost drivers · Export to PDF ·
Stay in control of your data

**Release notes:** First internal test release with offline ownership-cost calculations, six
templates including motorcycles, scenario analysis, comparison, PDF sharing, accessible themes and
languages, and optional cloud features.
